Dress Code

The dress code is semi-formal: suits for gentlemen and long dresses or jumpsuits for ladies.

Shuttle Service

We will be providing a small shuttle van. Pickup will be in front of the Estes Park Holiday Inn at 3:15 and will drop off at the chapel. After the ceremony, the shuttle will pick up from the chapel and drop off at the reception. The last shuttle from the reception back to the hotel will be at 8 pm.

Lodging

There are no reserved hotel blocks. The closest hotel to the reception is the Holiday Inn Estes Park. For those wishing to minimize their time at high elevation, we recommend staying 55 minutes away in Loveland.

Additional Questions

We hope to celebrate with you! If you have a specific concern or question, please get in touch with the bride, groom, or either of their parents.

Driving Directions and Parking

Cellular reception at the chapel is poor to non-existent. Please download your driving directions before you go! There is parking right next to chapel and additional overflow parking up the dirt road towards Tahosa Coffee House.

Parking is abundant and free at the reception venue. The reception is on the lower level of the building and has its own entrance.
